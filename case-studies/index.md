# Build Case Studies

**Jedi Wright / Systems of Thought / UX Minds, LLC**

---

These case studies document three bodies of work from the Seam Stack project — a local-first architecture for governed data crossings. Each study is written to be readable without prior context, with technical depth available for those who want it.

The work was built AI-assisted, under a governed methodology. What that means in practice — how decisions were made, how outputs were validated, what got rejected and why — is part of what these studies document.

---

## The Three Studies

### [PC#7 — The Employment Seam](./pc7-employment-seam.md)
A reference implementation of a governed data boundary at employment transitions. The most mature build in the series: five prototype iterations, a full reference implementation, 27/27 tests passing. Documents what it takes to go from a design pattern to production-ready code under a rigorous governance framework.

**Themes:** local-first architecture · verifiable credentials · governed schema evolution · reference implementation

---

### [PC#8 — The Substrate-Crossing Seam](./pc8-substrate-crossing-seam.md)
A newer, harder problem: what happens when governed data crosses from a local-first substrate (Automerge + Keyhive) into a globally distributed one (AT Protocol / Bluesky)? The epistemic properties of the record change at the crossing — and the architecture has to be honest about what it can and cannot guarantee after that moment.

**Themes:** AT Protocol · cross-substrate governance · exposure claims · write-before-fire architecture

---

### [The Governance Methodology](./methodology.md)
The build framework itself — Session Harness, Counter-Pass, Survival Ledger, and the discipline that makes AI-assisted work defensible. Not a theoretical framework: a working methodology extracted from real build sessions, with the failure modes and recovery patterns included.

**Themes:** AI-assisted development · governed review · epistemic discipline · delivery-not-application

---

## What These Studies Are Not

These are not marketing materials. They document real decisions, real findings, and real limits. Where the architecture cannot make a guarantee, that is named. Where a claim is speculative, it is stamped as such.

The Full Personhood essay ([systemsofthought.com](https://systemsofthought.com)) is the theoretical argument. These case studies are the evidence base.
