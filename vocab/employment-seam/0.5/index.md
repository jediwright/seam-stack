# seam:EmploymentSeam 0.5

Vocabulary namespace for the employment seam pattern (PC#7 v0.5).

**IRI:** `https://jediwright.github.io/seam-stack/vocab/employment-seam/0.5#`

Defines the terms specific to the employment seam: `seam:Seam`,
`seam:SeamFiring`, `seam:gateCheckRecord`, `seam:agentCapabilityGrant`,
`seam:agentRevocationState`, `seam:aiProvenance`, and the controlled
vocabularies for participant roles, identity classes, and regulatory regimes.

Part of the [Seam Stack](https://www.systemsofthought.com/seam-stack/) — a
four-layer architectural pattern for local-first systems where the seam, not
the server, is the primary design surface.
